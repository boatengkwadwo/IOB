# RM101 - Module 1: Introduction to Research

**What is Research?** Systematic investigation to find new facts.

**Types:**
- Qualitative (Interviews)
- Quantitative (Numbers & Graphs)

**Research Process:**
1. Choose Topic
2. Problem Statement
3. Literature Review
4. Methodology
5. Data Collection

**Task:** Propose 3 research topics related to IT in Ghana.

# Module 2: Research Proposal Writing

**Structure of Proposal (Chapter 1-3):**

**Chapter 1: Introduction**
- Background
- Problem Statement (e.g., Poor network in IOB labs)
- Objectives: Main and Specific
- Research Questions
- Significance

**Chapter 2: Literature Review**
- Review past work by other authors
- Use APA referencing

**Chapter 3: Methodology**
- Research Design: Qualitative/Quantitative
- Population & Sample
- Data Collection: Questionnaire, Interview

**Sample Title:** "Impact of Practical Labs on IT Students Performance at IOB - A Case Study"

**Assignment:** Write Chapter 1 draft (2 pages).
